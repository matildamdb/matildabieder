Hello, I’m Matilda.

I am a student interested in developmental biology and science communication. I want to understand how things came to exsistance from a biological to a philosophical view and I want to explain these ideas to people outside the lab.

I am currently a Master's student at TU Dresden (2024–2027), where I currently work with at the Center for Synergy of System. I received my Bachelor's degree from the University of Hamburg in 2024, where I worked on axial proteins important for mitotic division in maize. Along the way, I studied abroad at the University of Strathclyde in Glasgow in 2022, and in 2026 I am spending time at Columbia University and Harvard University, where I measured deer mice carcasses for genetic analysis of reproductive age.

Outside of university, I travel (a lot): from a language school in Seoul in 2018, interrail trips around europe to a diving excursion in Egypt. I love museums and art, cooking, and cinema.
